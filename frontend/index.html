📜 Real Rules (classic)

  1. 📝 Slips are made: Raja 1000, Rani 800, Minister 700… Thief 0. Everyone gets one secret slip.
  2. 👑 The Raja reveals himself and calls out — "Who is my Minister?"
  3. 📜 The Minister (Police with 4 players) steps forward and guesses the thief.
  4. ✅ Correct guess → the Minister keeps his points, the Thief gets 0. ❌ Wrong guess → the Minister gets 0, and the Thief steals the Minister's points!
  5. 👑 Rani, Commander, Soldier… all keep fixed points. After all rounds, the highest total wins — Raja of Rajas! 🏆
  6. 🎲 Fair rotation: you never get the same slip twice in a game — except Raja, Rani and Minister (max 3 times each). Set Rounds = Players to try every role!

          ❓ How to play? (school style)

          1. The host presses Create (4–24 players, up to 24 rounds) and shares the code.
          2. Everyone joins. Once there are at least 4, the host presses Start.
          3. Each round deals secret slips: Raja 1000, Rani 800, Minister 700, Commander 600, Soldier 500, Guard 400, Citizen 300, Villager 200, Helper 100, Thief 0 (top slips for the player count + Thief).
          4. With 4 players the guesser is the Police 500. With 5+ it is the Minister 700.
          5. 👑 The Raja presses "Who is my Minister?" → the Minister steps forward → pick a suspect.
          6. Correct = the Minister is safe, the Thief gets 0. Wrong = the Minister gets 0, and the Thief steals the Minister's points!
          7. Fixed roles always keep their points. The highest total at the end wins 🏆
          8. 🎲 Fair rotation keeps it fresh: no repeat slips (Raja/Rani/Minister max 3× each).
